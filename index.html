Ready to Become our Next New Alpha?

Joining the Alpha Code family is an exciting journey, and we're here to guide you every step of the way.

Here's how our application process works:

1. Submit Resume
and Application

Start by submitting your resume and application for the position you’re interested in. We’re always on the lookout for talented individuals who are passionate about what we’re doing and eager to make a difference.

2. Culture Interview

If your application catches our eye, we’ll invite you to a culture interview. This is where we get to know you better and ensure you’re a good fit for our dynamic and collaborative work environment.

3. Technical Interview

Next, we’ll conduct an interview to test your technical abilities. We want to see your skills in action and ensure that you’re ready to tackle the challenges that come with being a Alpha.

4. Possible In-Person Interview

Depending on the position and circumstances, we may schedule an in-person interview to further assess your fit for the role and the team.

5. Decision

After thorough evaluation and consideration, we’ll make a decision and welcome you aboard as a brand new Alpha! We understand how important this decision is, and we’ll make every effort to keep you informed throughout the process.

6. Alpha Code Academy

You will attend a two-day onboarding event to get your tech setup, work through any paperwork, learn about benefits, and get demos of our product.

At Alpha Code, our applicants are potential members of our thriving community. We value diversity, creativity, and a passion for excellence. We're excited to see how you can contribute to our mission of empowering people to make informed decisions.

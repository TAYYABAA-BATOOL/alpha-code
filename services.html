PLUGIN DEVELOPMENT

Plugins provide a powerful way to customize Alpha Core, such as integrating with other applications and creating new innovative ways to interact with your data.

If you need help getting started, our team can work side by side with your organization to build plugins to meet your unique requirements. Our team will manage the project, build beautiful designs, code plugins, and support deploying the new functionality to your workforce.

Whether you need specialized data visualization tools or bespoke integrations with other systems, we’ll deliver a robust and scalable solution!

DATA AGGREGATION

We offer different ways of getting data and organizing it for you. The core of this is data aggregation.

We offer Reality Capture Conversion to take CAD models and any form of data and combine them into a lightweight product model. All the data is in a relational format and easily accessible through Alpha Core.

Utilizing advanced scanning technologies, our team converts two-dimensional technical documents into detailed 3D representations with high accuracy and fidelity.

DEPLOYMENT SUPPORT

Since the inception of Alpha Code, we’ve been helping organizations aggregate and visualize their data in meaningful ways. These projects range from aggregating data across several different data sources to building data-rich 3D environments using CAD and reality capture.

We’ll start by working with your team to understand what information your organization is using. We’ll put together a strategy based on the types of data, where it’s hosted, the applications you use, and your desired goals.

The result will be a lightweight product model, where all your data is in a relational format accessible through Alpha Core.

User/Developer TRAINING

One of the biggest obstacles to any successful implementation is resistance to change.

Our hands-on training provides new users with an overview of the Alpha Core features and dives into advanced concepts, making your workforce an expert in data aggregation.

Training can be provided remotely or in-person based on your organization’s preference.

Through a tailored curriculum, you’ll learn how to:

  • Effectively use out-of-box features to interact with different types of data.
  • Import 3D models, documents, and metadata.
  • Create interactive learning experiences through Alpha Core’s drag-and-drop editor.
  • Build your own customizations using Alpha Core’s standards and plugin architecture.

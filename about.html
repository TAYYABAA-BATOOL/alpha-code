It all started around a card table ...

Our story at Alpha emerged from the collaboration of seven founders around a folding table.

The journey started working with the US Navy to visualize the design for a new ship. During this time, we practically lived with the customer!

As more people started to use the app, we realized our platform could solve a ton of problems. It allowed engineers and analysts to understand the design before the real-life asset was built. Operators could become qualified to execute complex tasks by doing them virtually. And the skilled workforce executing maintenance had all the data they needed to do their jobs.

We listened, we learned, and we iterated…over and over again…until we built ‘Alpha’ Core.

We grew as the market shared our excitement

Our customers and their success are the key to our growth.

Starting with the Navy, our customers and users introduced us to new organizations and markets facing the same challenge. How do we interrelate a ton of different data formats, spread across different IT systems, so our workforce doesn’t spend all day looking for the information they need?

With our technology, we brought together siloed data sources by creating digital threads across 3D models, documents, and metadata. And equipped workers with our Alpha Core software to quickly swap between different types of data and search for the information they need.

Today we have hundreds of professionals ready to help you

We’ve helped organizations big and small overcome the challenge of digital transformation. Our team specializes in understanding complex datasets, linking together enterprise systems, working with users to build intuitive workflows, and getting solutions into the hands of your workforce.

We are Alpha Code, and we’re ready to partner with you to make your mission a success!
